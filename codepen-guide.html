Build your About Me website!

A guide for families: set up a free CodePen account, make your own copy of our template, and fill it with all about you.

💻 Any computer with a web browser🆓 Free👨‍👩‍👧 Kids + grown-ups together

👋 Grown-ups, read this first

  • Websites made on free CodePen accounts are public, so anyone with the link can see them.
  • Use a first name or nickname only. Leave out last names, school names, addresses and photos of your child’s face.
  • Sign up with a parent’s email, and pick a username that doesn’t include your child’s full name.
1

Get into CodePen

Pick the box that fits you.

🌱 New to CodePen?

  1. Go to codepen.io and click Sign Up.
  2. Follow the steps using a parent’s email.
  3. Check that email inbox for a message from CodePen and click the link to verify the account. Check spam if you don’t see it.

🔑 Already have an account?

  1. Go to codepen.io and click Log In.
  2. Sign in the same way you did before.
  3. That’s it! Go to Part 2.
2

Make your own copy

This is called a “fork.” It copies our template into your account.

  1. Make sure you’re logged in, then open the template:
    Open the SBCS template →
    codepen.io/editor/SBCSstudent/pen/01a1f41a-f705-7c3e-9c9a-d1f9cddd9fed
  2. Click the Pen Actions menu (the ••• button), then click Fork.
  3. Your copy opens with the same name but its own new link. That one is yours to change!

Tip: if you start typing before you fork, CodePen will ask you to fork so your changes aren’t lost. Say yes.

3

Make it all about you

Change the sample words to your own.

index.html

The words and pictures on your website. You’ll spend most of your time here.

style.css

How your website looks: colors, sizes and shapes.

✏️ First: put in your name

Open index.html. Find [insert first name] and type your first name or nickname instead. It shows up in three places:

🌟The big hello at the top
👋The “All About Me” card
💙The “Made with 💙 by” line at the bottom
Example 
<!-- Before -->
<h1>Hi, I'm [insert first name]!</h1>

<!-- After -->
<h1>Hi, I'm Maya!</h1>

⭐ Golden rules for editing

  • Look for the gray notes that say <!-- EDIT: ... -->. They point to the parts you change.
  • Only change the words between the tags. Leave the < > parts and quote marks alone.
  • Made a mistake? Press Ctrl+Z (or ⌘+Z on a Mac) to undo.

✅ Then fill in each section

  • Welcome message at the top
  • All About Me: your grade and 3 words about you
  • My Favorites: food, animal, game and color
  • Things I Love to Do: 3 activities
  • My Picture Gallery: 3 pictures with captions
  • When I Grow Up: your dream job and a picture
  • Me + AI: answer the 3 questions
  • Bonus: change the website title near the top of the file

🖼️ Adding a picture

  1. Find a picture online (not of yourself).
  2. Right-click it and choose Copy image address.
  3. In index.html, replace PASTE-IMAGE-LINK-HERE with what you copied. Keep the quote marks!
<img src="https://…/puppy.jpg" alt="My favorite animal">

🎨 Bonus: change the colors

Open style.css. Colors are written as codes like #2f80ed (blue) and #ffd23f (yellow).

  1. Search “color picker” in a new tab and pick a color you love.
  2. Copy its code (it starts with #).
  3. Swap it in for one of the codes and watch your website change!
4

Save and share

Don’t lose your hard work!

💾 Save often

Press Ctrl+S (or ⌘+S on a Mac) every few minutes.

To find your website later, log in and go to codepen.io/your-work.

🎉 Show it off

Copy the link from your browser’s address bar and share it with family. Remember: anyone with the link can see it.

🛟 If something goes wrong

I can’t save my changes
You’re still on the SBCS template. Fork it first (Part 2), then make your changes in your copy.
My website looks broken
Press Ctrl+Z (⌘+Z) to undo until it looks right again. A missing <, > or quote mark is usually the cause.
My picture shows a broken icon
Try a different picture. Make sure you chose “Copy image address,” not the page address.
I can’t find my website
Log in, then go to codepen.io/your-work.
The verification email never came
Check the spam folder of the email you signed up with.
